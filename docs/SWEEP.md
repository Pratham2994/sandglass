# Sandglass spread sweep

Generated by `npm run sweep`. Every element that burns or explodes (not gases or living things) is built as a long one-cell strip (88 cells) and as a solid block, lit at one end the way it is meant to be lit (a flame, or great heat when a flame cannot light it), and watched for 20 s. A strip whose fire stops moving for 3 s while fuel is still ahead is flagged as **stalled**: that is the bug this sweep exists to catch (a C4 strip that only went off near the spark, a gunpowder trail that died). "Still spreading" is a slow but steady fire, which is realistic for wood or paper. Elements that melt or char before they burn (wax, plastic, cooked meat) show as stalled or 0% when nothing burns: check those against their descriptions.
