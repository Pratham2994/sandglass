      World rules

      What holds everywhere, for every element.

      • Room temperature is 20 °C. Empty cells are air. Air slowly drifts back to room temperature, which is how hot things cool and steam condenses.
      • Air feeds fire with oxygen strength 0.20999999344348907. Burning turns touching air into smoke, which feeds nothing, so a sealed space runs out of air.
      • Edges: the bottom and sides are walls. Smoke leaves through the top edge; other gases stay.
      • Gravity is per element: each falling element has its own acceleration and top speed.
      • Heat flows by touch. Each element has a conductivity and a heat capacity. Burning cells also push heat into their neighbours.
      • Nothing appears from nothing. Movement only swaps cells. Only reactions, burning and phase changes turn one element into another.
      • Phase changes reverse into their source: whatever an element melts or boils into sets back into that same element.
